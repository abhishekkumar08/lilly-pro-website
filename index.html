Directions

Simple to use,
safe to dispose

Four easy steps for a comfortable, hygienic experience every time.

1

Peel & Remove

Remove the release paper from the back of the pad to expose the adhesive.

2

Stick Firmly

Stick the pad firmly onto the centre of your panty, adhesive side down.

3

Fold Wings

Fold the stretchable wings around the panty and press firmly to secure.

4

Wrap & Dispose

Wrap used pad in a disposal bag and throw in a dustbin. Never flush pads.

Ask us anything

How many pads are in one pack?

▼

Each pack contains 7 sanitary pads (6 pads + 1 free bonus pad). All are 280mm XL size with stretchable wings.

What is the price of one pack?

▼

One pack of 7 Lilly-Pro XL pads is priced at just ₹42 (MRP inclusive of all taxes) — only ₹6 per pad, making it one of the most affordable quality options available.

Are Lilly-Pro pads safe to use?

▼

Absolutely. Lilly-Pro pads are manufactured under strict hygiene protocols. 100% Hygiene and 100% Safe are our core commitments. The cottony touch material is gentle on skin.

How long can the pads be stored?

▼

Lilly-Pro pads can be stored for up to 3 years from the date of manufacture (MFD). Always check the batch number and MFD printed on the packaging before use.

How do I dispose of used pads responsibly?

▼

Always wrap used pads in a disposal bag and throw them in a dustbin. Never flush sanitary pads — this causes blockages and harms the environment. The outer packaging is recyclable.

Where are Lilly-Pro pads manufactured?

▼

Packed and marketed by RR Hygiene Products, Saguna, Danapur Cantt, Patna, Bihar-801503, India. Proudly Made in India.
